TasomachiVR - third party components
====================================

This mod ships binaries it did not build. They are redistributed unmodified.


UEVR - praydog
--------------
    UEVRBackend.dll
    UEVRPluginNullifier.dll

The universal Unreal Engine VR injector that TasomachiVR is a plugin for. It
does the work this mod does not: stereo rendering, headset and controller
tracking, and the reflection interface the plugin uses to reach the game.

    https://github.com/praydog/UEVR                     MIT


OpenXR loader - The Khronos Group
---------------------------------
    openxr_loader.dll

    https://github.com/KhronosGroup/OpenXR-SDK          Apache 2.0


OpenVR - Valve Corporation
--------------------------
    openvr_api.dll

    https://github.com/ValveSoftware/openvr             BSD 3-Clause


Tasomachi: Behind the Twilight is the property of its authors. This mod
contains none of the game's own files: it reads the game as it runs and
changes nothing on disk beyond its own folder and the two settings the
uninstaller puts back.
